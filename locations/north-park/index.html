Bacari North Park.

New Orleans, taken from the building

3823 30th St, San Diego, CA 92104 · Location page

The look

A New Orleans-style exterior, carried inside: yellow plaster, green shutters, wrought-iron railings, chandeliers, blue-and-white tile and a long tiled bar. Out back, a courtyard under striped canopies, with a wrought-iron stair.

Worth noting for Austin

The clearest example of a facade setting the style for the whole restaurant.
