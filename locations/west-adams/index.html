Bacari West Adams.

The original: a gallery of old paintings

2308 S Union Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90007 · Location page

The look

The first Bacari, opened in 2008 in a brick storefront. Pressed-tin ceilings and chandeliers over long rooms, walls hung edge to edge with vintage oil paintings, green wainscoting, and tufted rust and moss banquettes with mismatched pillows.

Worth noting for Austin

The art-covered walls, chandeliers and tufted banquettes are the indoor DNA most guests recognize as Bacari.
