Bacari Long Beach.

Stone arches and a rooftop under a retractable roof

5620 2nd St, Long Beach, CA 90803 · Location page

The look

Old-world Mediterranean: stone arches, plaster walls, wrought-iron chandeliers, red banquettes and checkered tile. Upstairs, a rooftop dining room with a retractable roof and awnings that open to the sky.

Worth noting for Austin

An example of a covered space that can open fully, one way to cover guests outdoors.
