Bacari West 3rd.

A whitewashed courtyard under one big tree

8030 W 3rd St, Los Angeles, CA 90048 · Location page

The look

A white-plaster courtyard built around a single large tree, with gravel underfoot, timber pergolas, mustard bistro chairs and old arched doors. Inside is dark beams, whitewashed brick, portraits and hanging bundles of dried herbs.

Worth noting for Austin

Shows how one great tree can carry a whole patio, much like the heritage oak out front in Austin.
