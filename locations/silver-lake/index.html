Bacari Silver Lake.

A jungle backyard on tiered decks

3626 Sunset Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90026 · Location page

The look

Most of the restaurant is outdoors: tiered wood decks and gravel terraces built in and around mature trees and bamboo, with string lights overhead, a bar that opens straight onto the patio, and carved antique doors. Inside is stone columns, hex tile and Persian rugs.

Worth noting for Austin

The closest model for the Austin backyard: decks stepped around big trees, an outdoor bar, and a lot of seating that still feels like a garden.
