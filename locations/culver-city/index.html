Bacari Culver City.

A dark-wood tavern with stained glass

3833 Main St, Culver City, CA 90232 · Location page

The look

Dark wood, red leather and brass: a long bar, stained-glass ceiling panels, chandeliers, Delft-style painted tile walls and a fox-hunt mural behind the beer taps. The blue storefront folds open onto a sidewalk patio.

Worth noting for Austin

A more formal, moodier interior, and a good example of a front that opens fully to the outdoor seating.
