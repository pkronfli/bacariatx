Bacari Beverly Hills.

Art Deco, taken from the building

9601 Brighton Way, Beverly Hills, CA 90210 · Location page

The look

An Art Deco building, with Deco touches inside: an arched, tiled back bar with wrought-iron stools, brick columns, patterned tile floors, painted tile, stained glass and a gold-tiled tunnel hallway. Outside, a striped awning and a sidewalk patio lined with flower boxes.

Worth noting for Austin

Another example of taking the style from the architecture rather than copying a past location.
