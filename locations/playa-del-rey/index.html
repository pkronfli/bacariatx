Bacari Playa del Rey.

A beach house by the ocean

6805 S Vista Del Mar Ln, Playa Del Rey, CA 90293 · Location page

The look

A small beach house across from the ocean: wood-plank walls, hanging plants, a ceiling lined with wine bottles over the bar, and window counters facing the sunset. Outside, a tented patio with turf and greenery.

Worth noting for Austin

Shows how a light tent and planting can turn a plain lot into an outdoor room.
