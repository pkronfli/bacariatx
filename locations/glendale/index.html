Bacari Glendale.

Gallery walls and a big sidewalk patio

757 Americana Way, Glendale, CA 91210 · Location page

The look

At the Americana at Brand. Inside is darker and more industrial, with walls of framed paintings, a hanging barrel light and a copper-topped bar. Outside, a large fenced sidewalk patio under umbrellas and trees.

Worth noting for Austin

Shows how far a lot of outdoor seating can stretch with simple fencing, umbrellas and trees.
