          To put this to scale

          This version uses Robert's video, Travis County parcel records for 1315 W 6th St, Google Maps and Earth imagery from December 2023, USGS lidar ground heights from 2017, and Patrick's walk-around videos from outside the fence on November 23, 2026. The building footprint (about 46 × 45 ft), the tree positions, the back alley and the neighboring buildings were traced from the satellite view to within roughly 5–10 ft. The fence follows the county parcel lines, not a survey. Robert's deck locations are interpreted from what he points at in the video. These would make it a measured model.

          1. The Matterport scan. The share link helps, but the downloadable files help most: the point cloud (E57) or 3D mesh (OBJ) and the measured floor plan, which Matterport sells as add-ons.
          2. A site survey with a tree survey, showing property and lease lines, ground levels, and each oak's position and trunk size. The landlord may already have one, and Austin's tree rules will require it anyway.
          3. Three heights: the upper deck floor above the lot, the existing back fence, and the ground floor above the lot.
          4. The lease boundary: exactly how much of the parking lot, including where the shipping containers sit, comes with the lease.
          5. Robert's check on this layout: whether the high, medium and low decks sit where he meant, round or square bar, and what happens to the back lot.
